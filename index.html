Classifies recorded disease status from a dataset. Not clinically validated.

A prediction you can question

CardioLens is a student project built on a public dataset of 70,000 health records. A model estimates whether cardiovascular disease was recorded for a person with similar readings, and SHAP shows which readings pushed that estimate up or down.

It describes patterns in old records. It does not diagnose, and it does not predict who will get ill in future.

How it works

Three steps, from your readings to a result you can read.

Step 1

Enter your readings

Age, height, weight, blood pressure, cholesterol, glucose and lifestyle answers.

Step 2

We check the values

Impossible numbers, such as diastolic pressure above systolic, are flagged instead of silently used.

Step 3

See the result and why

A model score plus the inputs that influenced it most. Blood pressure leads in our tests.
